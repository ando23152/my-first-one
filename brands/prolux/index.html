Prolux parts: common questions

Our Prolux list has 5 parts: 3 nozzles and tools and 2 clips, caps and covers. 5 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

What size hose do Prolux vacuums use?
Prolux backpack and canister vacuums mostly use 1-1/2 inch (38 mm) wands and tools, the common commercial size. Look up a specific model in the hose size finder.
Are these genuine Prolux parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a Prolux part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
