Milwaukee parts: common questions

Our Milwaukee list has 41 parts: 17 brushes, 14 nozzles and tools, 5 dust separators, 3 adapters and 2 clips, caps and covers. 41 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

What size hose do Milwaukee vacuums use?
Milwaukee's wet/dry vacs use 1-1/4, 1-7/8, and 2-1/2 inch hoses, and its stepped attachment line spans all three. Look up a specific model in the hose size finder.
Are these genuine Milwaukee parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a Milwaukee part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
