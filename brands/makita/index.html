Compatible parts for Makita dust extractors and wet/dry vacs

Makita's cordless and corded dust extractors use 1 inch and 1-1/4 inch hoses, with tool cuffs in 22, 24, and 38 mm. We make adapters that join Makita hoses to other brands' tools and ports, and step-downs between Makita's own cuff sizes.
