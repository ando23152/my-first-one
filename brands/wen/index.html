WEN parts: common questions

Our WEN list has 17 parts: 13 adapters, 2 dust separators and 2 brushes. 17 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

What size hose do WEN vacuums use?
WEN's dust collectors use 4 inch hoses, and its benchtop tools have 1-1/2, 2, 2-1/2, and 4 inch dust ports. Look up a specific model in the hose size finder.
Are these genuine WEN parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a WEN part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
