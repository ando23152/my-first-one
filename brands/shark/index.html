Shark parts: common questions

Our Shark list has 30 parts: 17 nozzles and tools, 11 brushes, 1 roller or wheel and 1 clip, cap or cover. 16 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

What size hose do Shark vacuums use?
Shark uprights, cordless sticks, and robot vacuums use brand-specific wands, latches, and attachments. Look up a specific model in the hose size finder.
Are these genuine Shark parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a Shark part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
