Compatible parts for DeWalt wet/dry vacs and dust extraction

DeWalt wet/dry vacs come in three hose families, 1-1/4, 1-7/8, and 2-1/2 inch, and the brand's dust extractors use their own 1-1/4 inch / 35 mm connector. We make adapters, nozzles, and fittings that bridge those sizes and fit DeWalt tools to other brands' hoses.
