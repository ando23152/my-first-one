Samsung parts: common questions

Our Samsung list has 23 parts: 13 clips, caps and covers, 4 nozzles and tools, 4 rollers and wheels, 1 brush and 1 adapter. 16 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

Which Samsung parts can you make?
Small plastic trim, caps, rollers, and clips on Samsung refrigerators, dishwashers, and stick vacuums break often and can be hard to buy alone. Browse the list above, or send a photo of the part you need.
Are these genuine Samsung parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a Samsung part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
