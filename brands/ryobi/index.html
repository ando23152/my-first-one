RYOBI parts: common questions

Our RYOBI list has 17 parts: 10 nozzles and tools, 5 brushes and 2 adapters. 16 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

What size hose do RYOBI vacuums use?
RYOBI's cordless and corded wet/dry vacs mostly use 1-1/4 and 1-7/8 inch hoses, with larger models at 2-1/2 inches. Look up a specific model in the hose size finder.
Are these genuine RYOBI parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a RYOBI part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
