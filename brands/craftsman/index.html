Craftsman parts: common questions

Our Craftsman list has 4 parts: 2 nozzles and tools, 1 brush and 1 adapter. 4 have a confirmed fit for the listed models; for the rest, send your model number and we check before we make it.

What size hose do Craftsman vacuums use?
Craftsman wet/dry vacs use 1-1/4, 1-7/8, and 2-1/2 inch hoses, with Pos-I-Lock fittings on the larger models. Look up a specific model in the hose size finder.
Are these genuine Craftsman parts?
No. They are compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make in Modesto, California, with a free reprint if one doesn't fit.
Can you make a Craftsman part that isn't listed?
Often, yes, if it's plastic and not safety-critical. Send the part or photos with a ruler and the model number. The measuring guide shows what to send.
