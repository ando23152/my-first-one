How pricing works

Most business jobs run from about $75 to $800 or more. Every job gets a written quote with the material, price, and turnaround before we print anything, and every part is covered by our free-reprint fit guarantee.

What sets the price

Six things we price on.

FactorHow it affects your price
Size and print timeBigger and taller parts take longer to print and use more material. Size is usually the biggest single factor.
MaterialPETG and PLA cost least. ASA, TPU, and carbon-fiber nylon cost more and take more care to print.
Design workA part we already have a model for costs least. Measuring and modeling a new part in CAD is a one-time charge.
QuantityThe design charge is paid once, so the price per part drops as quantity goes up. See small-batch manufacturing.
Fit precisionTight press or snap fits may need a test print before the final part.
TurnaroundStandard turnaround is in your quote. Rush jobs that jump the queue cost more.
Typical jobs

Where common jobs usually land.

Each is a rough guide within the $75 to $800+ range; your written quote is the real number.

Low end of the range

One replacement part we already model

A catalog part, such as a dust adapter or crevice tool, printed to order. No design charge.

Low to middle of the range

One new part from a broken original

Measure, model, and print one part, with a test fit if needed.

Middle of the range

A custom bracket, jig, or fixture

Modeled from your drawing or measurements, then printed in the right material.

Middle to high of the range

A small-batch run of 25 to 500 parts

One design charge, then a per-part price that drops with quantity.

Save money

Ways to lower the cost.

Send the original

The broken part, even in pieces, makes modeling faster than working from photos. See the measuring guide.

Order more at once

The design charge is paid once. Ordering spares now is cheaper than a second order later.

Buy ready-made

Some parts are already listed in our eBay store at a fixed price. Repeat customers can reorder by part ID.

Describe your part and we'll reply with questions or a written quote. Free reprint if it doesn't fit.
