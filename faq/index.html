Frequently asked questions

Quotes, pricing, turnaround, materials, and what we can and can't make.

When do you open?

We open January 1, 2027. You can send quote requests now, and we'll reply with questions or a written quote so your job is ready to start when we open.

Link to this answer

What should I send to get a quote?

The original part is best, even if it's broken. If you can't send it, send clear photos from a few angles with a ruler or calipers in the shot, plus the make and model of the equipment and any part number you can find.

Have a 3D file? Attach it. We accept STL, STEP, and most CAD formats.

Link to this answer

How much does a part cost?

It depends on the part's size, the material, and how many you need. Most business jobs run from about $75 to $800 or more. Every job gets a written quote with the material, price, and turnaround before we print anything.

Link to this answer

How long does it take?

Turnaround depends on whether the part needs to be measured and modeled from scratch or we already have the model. Your written quote states the turnaround before work starts. Repeat orders are faster because your model is already on file.

Link to this answer

What if the part doesn't fit?

We reprint it free. Send us your measurements or the part, and we'll adjust the model and make it right. That's our fit guarantee.

Link to this answer

Can you make a part from a photo?

Sometimes. Simple parts can be modeled from good photos with a measurement reference. Parts with precise fits, like hose adapters and clips, usually need the original or careful caliper measurements. See reverse engineering.

Link to this answer

Are printed parts as strong as the original?

Often, yes, and sometimes stronger, if the material and print orientation are right. We choose materials like PETG, ASA, or carbon-fiber nylon for the job and can thicken the spot where the original failed. We'll tell you honestly if a printed part isn't a good fit for your use.

Link to this answer

What parts won't you make?

We don't make parts where a failure could hurt someone: safety-critical, load-bearing, pressurized, electrical, braking or steering, child-safety, medical, pool-safety, or code-compliance parts. We also don't make food-contact parts.

Link to this answer

Do you make repeat orders and small batches?

Yes. We keep your part's model on file, so reorders match the first run. Five or five hundred, with no molds and no large minimums. See small-batch manufacturing.

Link to this answer

Where are you located, and do you ship?

We're in Modesto, California, and serve Modesto, Ceres, Turlock, Manteca, Tracy, and Stockton, including on-site measuring for parts that can't come off the machine. We also ship statewide.

Link to this answer

Are your parts genuine brand parts?

No. They're compatible aftermarket parts that we design and make. Brand names and part numbers are used only to show what a part fits.

Link to this answer

Can I buy parts without a quote?

Some ready-made parts are available in our eBay store (opens in a new tab). Everything else is made to order.
