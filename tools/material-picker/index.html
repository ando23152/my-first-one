    Heat decides first

    If a part sees heat, that narrows the choice fast. Learn about heat deflection temperature.

    Sun is the silent killer

    UV light makes many plastics brittle. Outdoors, ASA is the safe default. See UV resistance.

    Design matters too

    Wall count, infill, and print orientation change strength as much as material does.
