TPU (flexible filament)

Materials

A rubber-like printing material for seals, gaskets, bumpers, and flexible cuffs.

TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) is a flexible, rubbery plastic. Its hardness is measured on the Shore scale; 95A, a common grade, feels like a firm rubber wheel.

TPU stretches, absorbs impact, and grips. It's useful for hose cuffs, bumpers, feet, gaskets, and protective covers.

It can be combined with rigid parts, for example a rigid adapter body with a flexible TPU seal.

What it means for your part

If a part needs to seal, grip, or flex, like a hose cuff, we'll suggest TPU or a rigid part with a TPU insert.
