PETG

Materials

A tough, slightly flexible plastic that handles moisture and moderate heat. Our everyday choice for functional parts.

PETG (polyethylene terephthalate glycol) is a close relative of the plastic used in drink bottles. It's tougher and less brittle than PLA and holds up better to moisture and many chemicals.

PETG softens around 70 to 80 °C, so it's fine indoors, in shops, and in most equipment, but not right next to engines or heaters.

Its slight flexibility makes it good for clips, snap fits, brackets, and adapters that get bumped.

What it means for your part

Most of our replacement parts and dust collection fittings are printed in PETG unless the part sees high heat or strong sun.
