Clips and covers (replacement)

Parts

Small plastic parts that hold things closed or cover an opening, like battery doors, end caps, hose clips, and trim pieces.

Clips, latches, caps, and covers break easily and are often hard to buy on their own, so a whole assembly gets replaced for one small piece.

These parts depend on exact fits, so we model them from the original or careful measurements and test the snap or latch before delivery.

Common examples are battery covers for remotes, appliance handle end caps, hose and cable clips, and equipment covers.

What it means for your part

The broken part, even in pieces, is the best reference. Otherwise send clear photos with a ruler and the equipment's model number.
