Infill

Process

The internal structure inside a printed part. More infill means a heavier, stronger part; less means lighter and faster.

Most 3D-printed parts aren't completely solid. The outside walls are solid, and the inside is filled with a repeating pattern called infill, such as a grid, honeycomb, or gyroid.

Infill is given as a percentage. Around 15 to 25 percent is common for parts that see light loads. Structural parts often use 40 percent or more, or solid infill where bolts clamp down.

Wall count usually matters more for strength than infill percentage, because the outer walls carry most of the load.

What it means for your part

We set infill and wall count for how your part is used, so it's strong where it needs to be without wasting material.
