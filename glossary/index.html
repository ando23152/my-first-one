Manufacturing and replacement parts glossary

Plain-English definitions of the materials, fits, and manufacturing terms that come up when you order a custom or replacement part, and what each one means for your part.

Process

FDM (fused deposition modeling)

The additive manufacturing (3D printing) method we use: melted plastic filament is laid down one thin layer at a time to build a part.

Read more →

Infill

The internal structure inside a printed part. More infill means a heavier, stronger part; less means lighter and faster.

Read more →

Layer height

How thick each printed layer is, usually 0.1 to 0.3 mm. Thinner layers give smoother surfaces; thicker layers print faster.

Read more →

Print orientation

Which way a part sits on the printer. It sets where the layers run and therefore where the part is strongest.

Read more →

Wall count (perimeters)

How many solid outlines the printer lays around every layer. More walls make a part stiffer and more durable.

Read more →

Materials

ABS

A classic tough plastic used in many factory parts, with good heat resistance but poor UV resistance outdoors.

Read more →

ASA

A weather-resistant plastic that holds up to sun and heat. Our choice for outdoor and enclosure parts.

Read more →

Heat deflection temperature

The temperature where a plastic starts to bend under load. It's the key number for parts near heat.

Read more →

PA-CF (carbon-fiber nylon)

Nylon reinforced with carbon fiber. Our strongest, stiffest, most heat-resistant option for demanding parts.

Read more →

PETG

A tough, slightly flexible plastic that handles moisture and moderate heat. Our everyday choice for functional parts.

Read more →

PLA

An easy-to-print plastic that is stiff and accurate, but softens at about 55 °C. Best for prototypes and indoor parts.

Read more →

TPU (flexible filament)

A rubber-like printing material for seals, gaskets, bumpers, and flexible cuffs.

Read more →

UV resistance

How well a plastic stands up to sunlight without fading, cracking, or turning brittle.

Read more →

Fit

Fit types (clearance, slip, and press fit)

How tightly two parts go together: clearance fits slide freely, slip fits slide snugly, and press fits need force.

Read more →

Tolerance

How far a finished dimension can be from the drawing and still be acceptable, for example ±0.2 mm.

Read more →

Design

CAD (computer-aided design)

Software for designing parts as precise 3D models. Every part we print starts as a CAD model.

Read more →

Prototype

An early working version of a part or product, made to test its fit, function, or look before committing to production.

Read more →

Reverse engineering

Measuring an existing part and recreating it as a CAD model, so a new one can be made.

Read more →

STL file

The common 3D file format sent to a printer. It describes a part's surface as a mesh of triangles.

Read more →

Parts

Aftermarket part

A replacement part made by someone other than the original manufacturer, designed to fit the same equipment.

Read more →

Bracket (custom mount)

A part that holds one thing to another, such as a sensor to a frame, a scanner to a cart, or a tool to a wall.

Read more →

Clips and covers (replacement)

Small plastic parts that hold things closed or cover an opening, like battery doors, end caps, hose clips, and trim pieces.

Read more →

Enclosure (custom housing)

A case or housing that protects a device, sensor, or circuit board and mounts it where it's needed.

Read more →

Food-contact parts

Parts that touch food or drink. We don't make them, because printed parts are hard to keep sanitary.

Read more →

OEM part

A part made by, or for, the original manufacturer of your equipment.

Read more →

Organizer (custom holder or tray)

A tray, rack, caddy, or holder shaped to fit specific tools, parts, labels, or supplies, so everything has its place.

Read more →

Roller and wheel (replacement)

A small plastic roller, wheel, or glide that lets a rack, drawer, or tray move, often the first part to crack or wear.

Read more →

Shop-vac hose sizes (1-1/4, 1-7/8, and 2-1/2 in.)

The three common wet/dry vac hose diameters. Matching them is the key to dust collection adapters that fit.

Read more →

Production

Fixture

A custom holder that locks a part in a fixed position for assembly, inspection, or machining.

Read more →

Jig

A custom tool that guides a cutting or drilling tool so the same operation is repeated accurately.

Read more →

Small-batch manufacturing

Making parts in runs of a few to a few hundred, without the molds and minimum orders of mass production.

Read more →
