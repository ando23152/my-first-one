Tolerance

Fit

How far a finished dimension can be from the drawing and still be acceptable, for example ±0.2 mm.

Tolerance is the allowed variation in a dimension. A hole specified as 10 mm ±0.1 mm is acceptable anywhere from 9.9 to 10.1 mm.

3D-printed parts have small, predictable variations from layer lines, plastic shrinking as it cools, and the nozzle's width. Holes often print slightly undersize, so designers adjust for that.

Tight tolerances matter where parts slide, snap, or press together. Loose tolerances are fine for covers and organizers.

What it means for your part

Tell us which surfaces have to fit something, like a hose, shaft, or mounting hole. We size those features carefully and check them before the part ships.
