Fit types (clearance, slip, and press fit)

Fit

How tightly two parts go together: clearance fits slide freely, slip fits slide snugly, and press fits need force.

A clearance fit leaves a gap, so parts slide or rotate freely. A slip fit is snug but can still be pushed together by hand. A press fit (interference fit) is slightly oversized, so it takes force and holds by friction.

Hose adapters and tool ports usually need a snug slip fit or a light press fit, so they stay put without cracking the plastic.

Printed plastic flexes less than rubber, so press fits are designed with smaller overlaps than you might expect.

What it means for your part

For adapters and fittings, tell us what the part connects to. We design the fit to seal and hold without splitting.
