OEM part

Parts

A part made by, or for, the original manufacturer of your equipment.

OEM stands for original equipment manufacturer. An OEM part is the official replacement sold by the company that made your tool, appliance, or machine, or by its authorized suppliers.

OEM parts match exactly, but they can be expensive, backordered, sold only in large assemblies, or discontinued entirely.

OEM part numbers are also useful as a reference. They tell us exactly which part you need to replace.

What it means for your part

If you know the OEM part number, include it. It helps us identify the part quickly, even when the original isn't available anymore.
