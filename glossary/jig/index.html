Jig

Production

A custom tool that guides a cutting or drilling tool so the same operation is repeated accurately.

A jig holds a part and guides a tool, for example a drilling guide that puts holes in exactly the same spot on every piece.

Jigs speed up repetitive work and reduce mistakes, which is why they're common in shops, assembly lines, and installation work.

Printed jigs are light, quick to make, and easy to update when a product changes.

What it means for your part

If your team does the same cut, drill, or alignment over and over, a printed jig can make it faster and more consistent.
