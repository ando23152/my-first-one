Print orientation

Process

Which way a part sits on the printer. It sets where the layers run and therefore where the part is strongest.

Because FDM parts are built in layers, they are strongest along the layers and weakest where one layer meets the next. This is called anisotropy.

Print orientation is how the part is placed on the build plate. The same bracket printed lying flat can be several times stronger than one printed standing up, if the load would otherwise pull the layers apart.

Orientation also affects surface finish, how much support material is needed, and how accurate holes and round features come out.

What it means for your part

We orient every part so the load runs along the layers, not across them. It's one of the main reasons a printed part lasts.
