ABS

Materials

A classic tough plastic used in many factory parts, with good heat resistance but poor UV resistance outdoors.

ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) is the plastic in many molded products, from tool housings to car interiors. It's tough, handles heat to roughly 95 °C, and can be sanded or smoothed.

ABS breaks down and yellows in sunlight, so for outdoor parts ASA is usually the better choice.

Like ASA, it needs an enclosed printer to avoid warping.

What it means for your part

If your original part was ABS and stays indoors, ABS or PETG is usually a good replacement. Outdoors, we'll suggest ASA.
