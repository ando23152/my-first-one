Small-batch runs: Five or five hundred.

Repeat production without molds or large minimums. We keep your part's model on file, so the next run matches the first. Free reprint if it doesn't fit.

Request a quote →How pricing works

What we make and how it works

Good for

  • Parts you need in the tens or hundreds, not thousands
  • Products still changing, where a mold would lock you in
  • Spare parts kept on hand for a line or fleet
  • Bridge production while a mold is being made

How it works

  • We model the part once, or use your file
  • A first article is checked against your fit dimensions
  • The run is printed and checked
  • Reorder any time by part ID

Good to know

  • No tooling cost and no 1,000-unit minimum
  • Price per part drops with quantity
  • Repeat orders match the first run
