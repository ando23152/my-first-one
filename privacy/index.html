Privacy

What happens to the information you share with us.

What we collect

This website doesn't use accounts, tracking cookies, or advertising. The quote form doesn't send anything by itself: it prepares a message that you choose to send from your own email.

When you email us, we receive what you include, such as your name, company, contact details, part description, photos, and files.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer your request, prepare quotes, make and deliver your parts, and keep records for repeat orders and our accounts. We don't sell or rent your information.

Files and part designs

Part photos, measurements, and models you send are used for your job and kept on file so we can reprint your part. Tell us if you'd like them deleted.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address. Links to our eBay store take you to eBay, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: nsottero@gmail.com.
