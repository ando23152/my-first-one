Shop vac adapter fit problems and fixes

Loose, tight, won't lock, or leaking: what causes each fit problem between hoses, ports and accessories, and how to fix it.

  1. It's loose or falls off
  2. It won't go on
  3. It fits but won't lock
  4. It leaks suction
  5. Two different sizes
  6. When not to adapt

It's loose or falls off

The male end is a little too small for the female end. Measure both: the outside of the part that slides in and the inside of the part that slides over (OD and ID). Tape is a short-term fix; it peels and leaks. A cuff sized to your exact port, or a flexible TPU sleeve, holds properly.

It won't go on

The parts may be the same nominal size but different real diameters, or one end is tapered the wrong way. Don't force it; plastic cracks. Measure both ends at the tip and the base, and compare them with the inch–mm converter.

It fits but won't lock

Brands use different locking ends. A same-size accessory from another brand will often slide on but not lock. That's fine for light use; for a secure joint, use an adapter with the right lock on one side and a friction fit on the other.

It leaks suction

Gaps at a joint waste suction and spread fine dust. Check for a cracked cuff, a worn friction fit, or a step adapter used between steps. A step adapter only seals well at the step that matches your port.

Two different sizes

Use Will it fit? to compare two models, then the adapter builder to request an adapter sized to both. Common sizes are listed in the size chart.

When not to adapt

Adapters are for vacuum and dust collection connections. We don't make pressurized, electrical or safety-critical fittings; see what we won't make.
