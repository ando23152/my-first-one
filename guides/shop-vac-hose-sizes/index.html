The three common sizes

Most wet/dry vacuums use one of three nominal hose sizes: 1-1/4 inch on small and handheld vacs, 1-7/8 inch on mid-size vacs, and 2-1/2 inch on large shop vacs. Woodworking dust collectors step up to 4 inch. Some brands, especially for dust extractors and commercial vacuums, use metric sizes such as 32, 35, or 38 mm.

The size names are nominal. A "2-1/2 inch" hose does not measure exactly 2.5 inches anywhere, and actual cuff and port diameters differ between brands. That is why a "universal" adapter often fits loosely or not at all. See shop vac hose sizes in the glossary.

How to measure your hose or port

Measure across the end with calipers if you have them, or a ruler with millimeter marks. For a hose cuff that slides into something, measure the outside. For a port that a hose slides over, measure the outside of the port; if the hose slides into it, measure the inside. Note whether the end is straight or tapered, and measure both ends of a taper. The measuring guide walks through it with photos.

Connecting two different sizes

A step adapter has several diameters and can be cut down to fit, which works for a quick fix. A custom adapter is sized to the exact hose and port you own, so it fits snugly on both without tape. The adapter builder lets you pick both ends and request one, and fit types explains slip and press fits.
