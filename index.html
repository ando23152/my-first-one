How a job runs

From broken to built.

Every job follows the same six steps, so you know what you're getting and what it costs before we print anything.

  1. 01

    Tell us

    Send photos, the broken piece, a sketch, or the model number.

  2. 02

    We measure

    Calipers in the shop, or on site for parts that can't come off.

  3. 03

    We model

    Drawn in CAD, matched to the original or improved where it failed.

  4. 04

    You approve

    Material, price, and turnaround in writing before work starts.

  5. 05

    We print

    Printed, cleaned up, and checked against the fit dimensions.

  6. 06

    It fits

    Delivered or shipped. If it doesn't fit, we reprint it free.

Materials & shop

Matched to where it lives.

A sunny truck cab, a cold warehouse, or behind a hot motor. We choose the material for the job and recommend it in your quote.

MaterialGood forTypical heat limitNotes
PLA / PLA-CFPrototypes, fit checks, indoor organizers≈ 55 °CStiff and accurate; not for heat or sun
PETGClips, brackets, everyday functional parts≈ 70 °CTough, slightly flexible, moisture resistant
ASA / ABSOutdoor, sun-exposed, and enclosure parts≈ 90–100 °CUV-stable (ASA); printed in an enclosed printer
PA-CF (nylon carbon)Structural, wear, and high-load parts≈ 150 °C+Our strongest option for demanding applications

Heat limits are typical published ranges and vary by brand and print settings. See heat deflection temperature.

About us

True Part Fabrication is a California manufacturing and product development company. We combine technology, craftsmanship, and customer-focused service to make useful parts more accessible and reduce unnecessary replacement costs.

Founded by three partners, we specialize in precision additive manufacturing, functional replacement parts, custom fabrication, and new product solutions. Over time we plan to grow into electronics, proprietary products, and advanced manufacturing.

Outside our scope

We don't make parts where a failure could hurt someone. If your part falls in one of these groups, we'll tell you and point you elsewhere.

  • Safety-critical, life-safety, or load-bearing parts
  • Pressurized or air-pressure parts
  • Electrical parts and housings
  • Automotive braking and steering
  • Child-safety, medical, and pool-safety items
  • Code-compliance parts and food-contact parts
