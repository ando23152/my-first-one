How a job runs

From broken to built

Every job follows the same path, so you know what you are getting and what it costs before we print anything.

  1. STEP 1

    Tell us the problem

    Send photos, the broken piece, a sketch, or the model number of what it came from.

  2. STEP 2

    We measure

    We take dimensions with calipers, or visit your site for parts that can't come off the machine.

  3. STEP 3

    We model it

    The part is drawn in CAD, matched to the original or improved where it failed.

  4. STEP 4

    Written quote

    Material, quantity, price, turnaround, and any limitations, in writing before work starts.

  5. STEP 5

    Print & check

    We print, clean up, and check fit dimensions against the quote before it leaves the shop.

  6. STEP 6

    Deliver & follow up

    Local delivery or shipping, then a check-in to confirm the part fits. If it doesn't, we reprint it free.

Outside our scope

We don't make parts where a failure could hurt someone. If your part falls in one of these groups, we'll tell you and point you elsewhere:

  • Safety-critical, life-safety, or load-bearing parts
  • Pressurized or air-pressure parts
  • Electrical parts and housings
  • Automotive braking and steering
  • Child-safety, medical, and pool-safety items
  • Code-compliance parts and food-contact parts
