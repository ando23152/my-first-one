What this part does

Clips, caps, covers and holders are small parts that crack, snap or get lost, and are often hard to buy on their own. This one is made to the same shape and fit as the Samsung DD61-00497A.

Will it fit?

We haven't confirmed this part on a real Samsung machine yet. Send your model number, or a photo with a ruler, and we'll confirm the fit before we make it. Not sure how to measure? Use the measuring guide.

Material

Made in ASA. ASA resists sunlight and heat better than most plastics, so it suits parts used outdoors or in hot spaces. Also available in PA-CF; tell us in your request. Compare materials in the material picker.
