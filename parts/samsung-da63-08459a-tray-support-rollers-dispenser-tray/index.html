Ask a question

Send your equipment's model number with the request so we can confirm the fit before we print. Not sure of a size? Use the measuring guide. Prices and turnaround are in your written quote; see the FAQ and our Terms of Service.

What this part does

Rollers and wheels wear out or break long before the rest of the machine. This one is made to the same size and fit as the Samsung DA63-08459A.

Will it fit?

Confirmed to fit: RF23HCEDBSG, RF28HFEDBSG, RF28HFEDTSG. Not on the list? Send your model number with the request and we'll check before we make it.

Material

Made in ASA. ASA resists sunlight and heat better than most plastics, so it suits parts used outdoors or in hot spaces. Also available in PA-CF; tell us in your request. Compare materials in the material picker.

Questions

Is this a genuine Samsung part?
No. It is a compatible aftermarket part that we design and make in Modesto, California.
What if it doesn't fit?
Tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll adjust it and make a new one at no charge. See the Terms of Service.
Can I order more than one?
Yes. Set a quantity in your cart, or ask about small-batch runs for larger orders.
