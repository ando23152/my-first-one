What this part does

A nozzle or tool attachment goes on the end of a wand or hose for a specific cleaning job. This one is made to the same connection as the Samsung VCA-SAR95/AA, so it attaches the way the original does.

Will it fit?

Confirmed to fit: Samsung Jet stick (models not listed). Not on the list? Send your model number with the request and we'll check before we make it.

Material

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Compare materials in the material picker.
