What this part does

A brush attachment goes on the end of a wand or hose for dusting and loosening debris. This one is made to the same connection as the RIDGID LA2501, so it attaches the way the original does.

Will it fit?

Confirmed to fit: RIDGID 2-1/2 in. vacs. Not on the list? Send your model number with the request and we'll check before we make it.

Connection size: 2-1/2 in (see how hose and port sizes are measured). Every RIDGID hose, wand and port size we know is in the hose size finder.

Material

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Compare materials in the material picker.

Questions

Is this a genuine RIDGID part?
No. It is a compatible aftermarket part that we design and make in Modesto, California.
What if it doesn't fit?
Tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll adjust it and make a new one at no charge. See the Terms of Service.
Can I order more than one?
Yes. Set a quantity in your cart, or ask about small-batch runs for larger orders.
