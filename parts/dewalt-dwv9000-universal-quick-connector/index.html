What this part does

An adapter joins two hoses, wands, tools or ports that don't connect on their own. This one is made to the same connection sizes as the DeWalt DWV9000, so it fits where the original does.

Will it fit?

We haven't confirmed this part on a real DeWalt machine yet. Send your model number, or a photo with a ruler, and we'll confirm the fit before we make it. Not sure how to measure? Use the measuring guide.

Connection size: 1-1/4 in, 35 mm (see how hose and port sizes are measured). Every DeWalt hose, wand and port size we know is in the hose size finder.

Need two sizes this part doesn't join? The adapter builder makes one to order, and the guide to fit types explains slip-over versus slide-in ends.

Material

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Also available in PA-CF; tell us in your request. Compare materials in the material picker.

Questions

Is this a genuine DeWalt part?
No. It is a compatible aftermarket part that we design and make in Modesto, California.
What if it doesn't fit?
Tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll adjust it and make a new one at no charge. See the Terms of Service.
Can I order more than one?
Yes. Set a quantity in your cart, or ask about small-batch runs for larger orders.
