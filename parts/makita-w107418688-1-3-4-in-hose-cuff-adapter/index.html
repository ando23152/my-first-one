What this part does

An adapter joins two hoses, wands, tools or ports that don't connect on their own. This one is made to the same connection sizes as the Makita W107418688, so it fits where the original does.

Will it fit?

Confirmed to fit: Makita VC4210L dust extractor (1-1/4 in. hose). Not on the list? Send your model number with the request and we'll check before we make it.

Connection size: 1-3/4 in (see how hose and port sizes are measured). Every Makita hose, wand and port size we know is in the hose size finder.

Need two sizes this part doesn't join? The adapter builder makes one to order, and the guide to fit types explains slip-over versus slide-in ends.

Material

Made in TPU. TPU is a flexible, rubber-like material. It grips, seals and bends without cracking. Compare materials in the material picker.
