What this part does

Dust separators and collectors catch dust and debris before it reaches the vacuum, filter or work area. This one is made to the same fit as the DeWalt DWH304DH.

Will it fit?

We haven't confirmed this part on a real DeWalt machine yet. Send your model number, or a photo with a ruler, and we'll confirm the fit before we make it. Not sure how to measure? Use the measuring guide.

Material

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Compare materials in the material picker.
