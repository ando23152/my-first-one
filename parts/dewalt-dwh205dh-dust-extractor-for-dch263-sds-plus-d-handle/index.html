What this part does

Dust separators and collectors catch dust and debris before it reaches the vacuum, filter or work area. This one is made to the same fit as the DeWalt DWH205DH.

Will it fit?

Confirmed to fit: DCH263. Not on the list? Send your model number with the request and we'll check before we make it.

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Compare materials in the material picker.
