What this part does

A nozzle or tool attachment goes on the end of a wand or hose for a specific cleaning job. This one is made to the same connection as the Shop-Vac 9196600, so it attaches the way the original does.

Will it fit?

We haven't confirmed this part on a real Shop-Vac machine yet. Send your model number, or a photo with a ruler, and we'll confirm the fit before we make it. Not sure how to measure? Use the measuring guide.

Connection size: 1-1/4 in, 1-1/2 in (see how hose and port sizes are measured). Every Shop-Vac hose, wand and port size we know is in the hose size finder.

Material

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Compare materials in the material picker.
