What this part does

Clips, caps, covers and holders are small parts that crack, snap or get lost, and are often hard to buy on their own. This one is made to the same shape and fit as the Hampton Bay UC7083T.

Will it fit?

Confirmed to fit: UC7083T remotes. Not on the list? Send your model number with the request and we'll check before we make it.

Material

Made in PETG. PETG is tough and slightly flexible, and it holds up to moisture and common cleaners, which makes it a good everyday material for vacuum and shop parts. Compare materials in the material picker.
