About these terms

These terms apply to quotes, orders, and parts from True Part Fabrication LLC ("we," "us"), a California company in Modesto. By sending a quote request or placing an order, you agree to them. Your written quote may add details for a specific job; if the quote and these terms differ, the quote controls for that job.

Quotes and orders

Every job starts with a written quote that states the part, material, quantity, price, and estimated turnaround. Work begins after you approve the quote.

Turnaround times are estimates. We'll tell you promptly if a job will take longer than quoted.

Payment terms, including any deposit, are stated in your quote.

Aftermarket parts

Our parts are aftermarket parts that we design and make. They are not original manufacturer (OEM) parts. Brand names and part numbers are used only to describe fit. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any brand we mention.

Using an aftermarket part may affect a manufacturer's warranty on your equipment. Check your warranty before installing one.

Parts we don't make

We don't make parts where a failure could hurt someone, including safety-critical, life-safety, or load-bearing parts; pressurized or air-pressure parts; electrical parts and housings; automotive braking or steering parts; child-safety, medical, or pool-safety items; code-compliance parts; and parts that contact food or drink.

We may decline any job, and we'll tell you why.

Your information and designs

You're responsible for the accuracy of the measurements, photos, drawings, and files you send, and for describing how the part will be used.

By sending us a part, drawing, or file, you confirm you have the right to have it reproduced or modified for your own use. We keep your part's model on file so we can reprint it for you; we don't sell it or make it for others without your permission.

Fit guarantee

If a part we make doesn't fit the equipment described in your quote, tell us within 30 days of delivery. Send your measurements or the part, and we'll adjust the model and reprint it at no charge.

The fit guarantee covers fit. It doesn't cover parts used outside the conditions described in your quote (for example, higher heat or load), parts that were modified after delivery, or problems caused by incorrect measurements or information you provided. In those cases we'll quote a corrected part.

Intended use

You're responsible for confirming that a part is suitable for your equipment and use, and for installing it correctly. 3D-printed parts have different properties from molded parts; we'll recommend a material, but final suitability is your decision.
